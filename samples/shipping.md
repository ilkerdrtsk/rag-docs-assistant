# Shipping

## Delivery times

Standard shipping within the European Union takes 3 to 5 business days. Express
shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and is available for orders placed before 14:00
CET. Orders to the United Kingdom and Switzerland take 4 to 7 business days because of
customs clearance.

## Costs

Standard shipping is free for orders over 60 EUR; below that it costs 4.90 EUR.
Express shipping costs 12 EUR regardless of order value. Bulky items such as tents
over 5 kg carry a 6 EUR surcharge.

## Tracking

A tracking link is emailed when the parcel leaves the warehouse. If tracking has not
updated for 5 business days, support opens an investigation with the carrier and ships
a replacement once the parcel is declared lost.
