# Returns and Refunds

Trailhead Supply Co. accepts returns of unused items within 45 days of delivery. Items
must be in their original packaging with all tags attached. Worn footwear, opened fuel
canisters and personalised products cannot be returned.

## Starting a return

Customers start a return from the "My orders" page by choosing the item and a reason.
The system emails a prepaid return label within one hour. Returns from outside the
European Union need a customs form, which support sends separately.

## Refund timing

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days after the
warehouse inspects the item. Inspection usually takes 2 business days from arrival.
Store credit is issued immediately and includes a 10% bonus.

## Exchanges

Size exchanges for the same product are free and ship as soon as the original item is
scanned by the carrier. Exchanges for a different product are handled as a return plus
a new order.
