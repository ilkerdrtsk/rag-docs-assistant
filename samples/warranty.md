# Warranty

## Coverage

All Trailhead-branded gear has a 3-year warranty against manufacturing defects such as
broken zippers, delaminating soles and failed seams. Normal wear, accidents and damage
from improper use are not covered.

## Making a claim

Warranty claims need the order number and photos of the defect. Customers submit them
through the warranty form; a technician reviews each claim within 7 business days.
Approved claims are repaired first; if repair is not possible, the item is replaced
with the same or an equivalent model.

## Third-party brands

Products from other brands follow the manufacturer's own warranty. Support helps
customers contact the manufacturer but cannot approve those claims.
